Terms of Service

General terms

  • Commission prices may be higher than listed depending on the complexity of the work requested.
  • I retain the right to reject an order for any reason.

Payment

  • Payments are done through BuyMeACoffee (linked above) and in USD. Cryptocurrency (Monero) is a second option.
  • I will first send a sketch when I accept the commission, and you may pay 50% of the total price as confirmation. When the work is completed, I will send a preview, and you may pay the remaining 50% as confirmation. You may request a refund up until the sketch stage.
  • Please do not pay until I have accepted the commission and given you a slot. I will provide an ID for the commission for you to use during payment. This ID will be used as the payment description or message.

Process and Delivery

  • The time it takes to deliver a commission varies by canvas size and complexity of components. The rule of that thumb is that more expensive works will take longer to deliver, but not more than 2 weeks. See the next point for details.
  • The time it takes to complete a commission may vary, but I can generally provide the preliminary sketch within a week, and the final product within the following week, depending on the number of commissions I have taken at a time. I may cancel the order if special circumstances occur, such as travel or illness, if they conflict with the deadline I have set. I will refund the order fully in that case.
  • When the commission is complete, I will email you the full-resolution image as a PNG in addition to your preferred lossless format. Commercial artworks will be sent in formats required by the use case.
  • Project files or separate layers and components may be shared for commercial uses if they are required.

Revisions

  • The preliminary sketch aims to verify expectations and requirements.
  • Minor changes (fixing errors, touching up the work, coloring adjustments, etc) to the completed commissioned work are free of cost. Major changes (such as recreating a rendered background or changing a pose) that take more than an hour to perform will cost up to a maximum of %50 of the base cost depending on the time it takes to make the change. If the change is a result of a misunderstanding on my end (i.e. misreading or forgetting the written directions), there will be no extra costs. To reduce communicative errors, I aim for accuracy and verification of details in the sketch stage.

  • I will most likely share the commission (low res) here on my portfolio for illustration purposes, unless instructed not to do so. Private commissions depicting real individuals will not be shared here.

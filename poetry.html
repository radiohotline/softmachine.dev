a state only the texture of a painting could capture

hazy memory and clouded emotion
blurred vision and blurred sense of self

disconnected

disorganized

dissociated

my circadian rhythm is the opposite of yours
ritualistic patterns i fail to comply with

when i can't sleep but can't wake up either,
i indulge in misplaced fantasy
and in the failed attempt to recollect fragmented ideas
that scurry away like squirrels

"what is your bona fides?"
"was your breakfast fried eggs with toast?"
"would you throw up at the sight of intestines strung out like a necklace?"

communication with myself is beyond esoteric
communication with others is incompetent and impulsive
communication through music is chaotic and raw
communication through art is insufficient

like reading a note that your brain left for you
that is smudged beyond legibility

i am not in my childhood photos
i am not in my mind
i am not in mirrors
i may be in others
mismatched, misbound, mistranslated

it will rain down again, after all, shimmering like sequin, slipping away like falling pages

"hello, can we talk?"

February 2022

of an exchange between two on a rainy night

Throw away your soggy cigarette and ask me about my day.
Take a seat and smear with your finger the fuzz
of the meniscus of cold broth in a bowl.

Magnetise your bi-polar heart and show me where it points.
Guide me to the sea and explain
what you'll first fend off against:
the salt or the seaweed.

Where would you go
if you were to leave a trail of footsteps in the snow,
and weren't sure whether you'd come back the same or different?

I want to dissect your mind and study your thoughts.
I will determine whether your spirit is as fragile as
a rose in vitro,
as a naïve heart,
as the truth,
or if it is as sturdy as
history,
as a violin string,
as glass—

February 2022

Once more

Once more, you came knocking on my door.
An even and predictable knock it was.
Every fortnight, more or less, you come.
A joy you instill that's worthy of applause.

But you leave.

Flowing animus, will thou aid me?
From me, embrace of hers slipped away again:
merciless, ruthless, rather graceful
departure. Love knows not of me — not of men.

But what if?

O episteme, will thou aid me?
Of no need is love that aches. Rationale knows.
In darkness, the wandering mind dies.
Only minds can fill other minds. Dispose!

Veracity.

What is virtuous in a patience that yields no fruit?
Perhaps, honour is in the heart of the resolute,
for only they have endured self-torture for an oath.
"The light of my life is worth more than my hurt," they quoth.

Once more, I came knocking at your door.

May 2025 - prompt: "space"

I ate my friend

I ate my friend today
It tumbled around my palm
I raised my hand and swallowed it
Sucking it in like medicine

Cruel hours pass before me
A shadow sleeps in the corner
A dog is walking on a leash
My bitten maw ulcerates

Later we’ll play and we’ll find out
What our endless chatter will be about
But that won’t happen, it is now clear
Because I ate my friend today

Tomorrow still, no one in sight
Not in my shit, not under light
If I ate it, we're tied like laces
A knot I tore with my hands

The Heat

Quadruplicity breeds invocation
The poke hangs shivering
Like a cordial hand offering
You an umbilical cord for your troubles
To rejoin the womb that fed you
To nourish every deprived cell in your
Bloodstream, screaming for the shot
The tourniquet tightens in anticipation
It twitches and turns and tries
It bursts
Like an unfolded book
Like a vacuole in a lymphocyte
Like a gangrenous leg at a train station
It bids hello
It greets goodbye
It treats a magnanimous hole in the skull of those whose fluids fly
High
The rush, it invades, it breathes
It pulses, it pushes, it stretches
It awakens you into a reverie
It calls for paléoanthropologie
The cannibalistic wish for a soul to settle
To settle into a flat bedding engraved
Engraved in the depths of the ocean that
Ocean that dries in the sun and brings
Brings to you a saline undercover boss
Boss of the schools that steal vacancies
Vacancies in which we know
Quadruplicity breeds invocation

May 2026

The Bullet

The pantomime of leaves in which you swirl in a yellow dress
Collapses when you run afar, diving into the dull air
The sun blares its scalding glow, incinerating the hearts
Of eleven figures erected in the earth, forming a wreath of bones around the moist mound of dirt
Their sweat collects in the wrinkles of their foreheads, and their tears settle in their eyelids
Their long black dresses are indistinguishable from their black veils
And their arms are reserved to those from which life emanates and laughs in their faces
You leap from stone to stone, fluttering like a hummingbird
You have long shed the lugubrious skin of a thousand years
And have become lighter than a speck of cotton that has escaped from its woven bedding
Your closed unclouded eyes lay heavy in their sockets
Having let in much of the world's lights yet fail to stir the whirlpool of despair
Borne of the burning hearts struggling to grab the conscience of their lost one
As it slips between their fingers

July 2026

How Dare The World Go On

How dare the world go on
When I am on my knees in the frozen glass
Entrapped by invisible ghosts

How dare the world go on
When I am pounding on the black curtains
Unable to move my legs

How dare the world go on
When I have thrust blades into my eyes
And cut my arms off

How dare the world go on
When content hearts and jovial spirits
Mock my silent cries

How dare the world go on
When the whole universe must lay still
Wallowing in a deathly wail
With me
